# Executive Insights & Strategic Recommendations — Healthcare Revenue Cycle Intelligence

**Prepared by:** Abraham Adetola, PMP®, PMI-PBA®, CSM®, CSPO®

## 1. Analytical Observations (Simulated Portfolio Benchmark Dataset)
- **Scope Clarity:** The default Oncology reference view captures `6,000` patient visits and `~50,000` line-item claims across 5 major payers in FY 2025, while the full multi-department warehouse spans `29,000` visits across Oncology, Cardiology, Orthopedics, General Medicine, and Emergency.
- **Payer Denial Concentration:** Across Oncology claims, **UnitedHealthcare** exhibits the highest denial rate (`~17%`), materially exceeding **Medicare**, **Blue Cross Blue Shield**, **Aetna**, and **Medicaid** (`~11%–13%`).
- **Dominant Denial Root Causes:** **Prior authorization**, **Coding / documentation**, and **Eligibility / coverage** consistently account for over 75% of all claim denials.
- **Collection Lag & Recovery:** Initial January clearinghouse ramp-up creates an early-year collection dip that stabilizes from March through December; targeted denial appeals recover meaningful revenue when escalated promptly.

## 2. Strategic Recommendations
1. **Prior Authorization Pre-Check Gate:** Deploy automated payer-specific prior-authorization rules ahead of high-cost Oncology and Surgical scheduling, prioritizing UnitedHealthcare.
2. **Mid-Cycle Clinical Documentation Integrity (CDI):** Institute pre-bill coding audits focused on the top two mid-cycle denial drivers.
3. **Structured Appeal SLA Governance:** Standardize 15-day appeal turnaround SLAs and track monthly overturn yield by payer.
4. **Q1 2026 Forecast Variance Reviews:** Reconcile actual Q1 2026 cash collections monthly against the 95% confidence corridor to detect payer adjudication delays early.

*(Note: Expected benefits require operational implementation and live EHR/clearinghouse measurement before claiming realized organizational savings.)*
